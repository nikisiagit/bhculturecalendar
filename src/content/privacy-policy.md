# BH Culture Calendar – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 11 November 2026

BH Culture Calendar ("we", "us") runs the BH Calendar iPhone app and bhculturecalendar.co.uk. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Questions: bhculturecalendar@gmail.com.

## No account needed

You can use the app without creating an account or signing in. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number in the app.

## Information the app uses

**Location (optional).** If you allow "While Using the App" location access, the app uses your precise location to show venues and events near you (Near Me) and, if you turn them on, to send alerts about nearby events. For nearby alerts, your location is sent to our server (api.bhculturecalendar.co.uk). You can turn location access off at any time in iPhone Settings.

**Notifications (optional).** If you allow notifications, the app registers an anonymous device identifier and an Apple push notification token with our server, together with your notification preferences (for example, new event alerts or the daily digest). This identifier is created by the app and isn't linked to your name or contact details. It's used only to send the notifications you've chosen.

**Calendar (optional).** If you choose to add an event to your calendar, the app asks for calendar access and adds only that event. We don't read or upload your calendar.

**Stored on your device.** Your app preferences are kept on your iPhone. Security keys used to talk to our server are stored in the iPhone Keychain.

**Event content.** The app downloads event listings and images from our server and from third-party websites that host event images. Those sites may receive standard technical information such as your IP address when an image loads, as with any web request.

**Links and the in-app browser.** When you open an event or venue link, the page loads in an in-app browser. Websites you visit there have their own privacy policies.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell your personal data.
- We don't use advertising or tracking SDKs, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
- We don't use third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app.

## How long we keep data

We keep your device identifier, push token, preferences and, if you use nearby alerts, the location you share only as long as you keep notifications or nearby alerts on. Data for devices that haven't contacted our server for 12 months is deleted.

## Your choices and rights

You can turn off location, notifications or calendar access in iPhone Settings at any time, and deleting the app stops all data collection. Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you can ask to access or delete data linked to your device by emailing bhculturecalendar@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## Children

The app is a general-audience events guide and isn't directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and post the new version on this page.
